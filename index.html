Who we are

One people. One purpose.

Mboho Mkparawa Ibibio (MMI) is a socio-cultural organisation of Ibibio young adults formed in Calabar on 1st August 1987, when Akwa Ibom State was part of the old Cross River State. The Lagos Chapter carries that heritage into Nigeria's busiest city.
