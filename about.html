Our story

Who is MMI?

Mboho Mkparawa Ibibio (MMI) is a socio-cultural organisation of Ibibio young adults formed in Calabar on 1st August 1987, when Akwa Ibom State was part of the old Cross River State. The Lagos Chapter carries that mission to Nigeria's commercial capital.


We believe members are entitled to dignity, fairness, justice, respect, responsibility and accountability.
